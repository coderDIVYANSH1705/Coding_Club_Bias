## 🔑 Environment Variables Needed

Copy these from your `.env.local`:
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`
- `ADMIN_ID`
- `ADMIN_PASS`
